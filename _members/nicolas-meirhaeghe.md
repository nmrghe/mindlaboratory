Nicolas Meirhaeghe [<i class="fa-solid fa-language"></i> "Mérag"] is a tenured CNRS researcher working at the [Institut de Neurosciences de la Timone](https://www.int.univ-amu.fr/en) (INT) within the Faculty of Medicine of [Aix-Marseille University](https://smpm.univ-amu.fr/en/research/research-themes/neurosciences). Initially trained as an aerospace engineer in [Supaéro](https://www.isae-supaero.fr/en/) and [Caltech](https://www.caltech.edu/), Nicolas received his PhD in computational systems neurosciences from MIT, working under the guidance of [Mehrdad Jazayeri](https://jazlab.org/) in the Harvard-MIT [Division of Health Sciences and Technology](https://hst.mit.edu/) from 2016 to 2021. He then completed his postdoctoral training as an EMBO and Marie Skłodowska-Curie Fellow in [Thomas Brochier](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=3oVxH0QAAAAJ&hl=fr)'s team at INT from 2021 to 2025, before being recruited as a CNRS researcher in 2025.
