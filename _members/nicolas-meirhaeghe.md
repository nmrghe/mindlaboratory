Nicolas Meirhaeghe ["Mérag"] is a tenured CNRS researcher working at the [Institut de Neurosciences de la Timone](https://www.int.univ-amu.fr/en) (INT) within the Faculty of Medicine of Aix-Marseille University. Initially trained as an aerospace engineer at Supaéro and Caltech, Nicolas received his PhD in computational systems neurosciences from MIT, working under the guidance of [Mehrdad Jazayeri](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=AkJyWbAAAAAJ&hl=en) from 2016 to 2021. He then completed his postdoctoral training as an EMBO and Marie Skłodowska-Curie Fellow in the team of [Thomas Brochier](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=3oVxH0QAAAAJ&hl=fr) at the INT from 2021 to 2025, before being recruited as a CNRS researcher in 2025.
