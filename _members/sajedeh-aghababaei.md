Sajedeh joined the lab as a PhD student through the EU-funded SCHADOC program. Her research uses dynamical-systems approaches and state-space analysis to investigate neural activity during motor planning and learning. During her studies in Biomedical Engineering (BSc, Tehran Polytechnic) and Neuroengineering (MSc, IUST), she focused on computational models of neural population activity and explored methods to decode hand movements from spiking activity. Outside the lab, She enjoys writing short stories, exploring cities on foot, and photographing urban life.
